# Moving the default model from Jev 1.13 to Jev 1.14

Registered 2026-10-15, before any 1.14 request. The maintainer asked to move to 1.14 ("bizde 1.14 modeline geçelim") and to run claude-referee everywhere so real use tests it. The move is still checked on the recorded suites first, because every threshold in the packs was set on 1.13 and TypeSafe's models page says an alias moves without a code change and that thresholds tuned on one version should be re-checked.

## Facts from the docs (read 2026-10-15)

- `jev-1.14.0`, available from 2026-10-14; same price as 1.13 (\$0.042 per million input tokens), same context (64k per request, 32k per question), text input.
- `jev-latest` still points to `jev-1.13.0`; `jev-preview` points to `jev-1.14.0`. Images work on 1.13 and 1.14; `Box` questions need 1.14.
- No 1.14 jaggedness page exists yet; the watched one is for 1.13.

## What is compared

The recorded suites that exercise the model: `done-v2` and its five later hold-outs, `done-v2-h6p`, `done-silent`, `done-vite`, `injection`, `verify-v2`, `claims-tr`, `decide-best`, `decide-close`, `decide`, `judge-env`, `judge-risky`, `stop-hard`, `stop-sessions`, `stop-study`, `stop-negation`. Scratch copies of `jev-evals` (the committed recordings are not touched): one fresh re-record of 1.13 (the re-ask noise), two fresh records of 1.14. Same packs, same thresholds, scored offline with `eval score`.

A first probe already shows the noise: re-recording the 17 sent cases of `done-v2-h5` on 1.13 moved `met` from 10 to 9 and recall from 0.90 to 0.81. So one run per model cannot separate a model difference from noise.

## Bars

- **B1 (no more wrong positives):** pooled wrong positives (wrong `met`, wrong `supported`, wrong yes) in each 1.14 run are at most the larger of the two 1.13 runs (the committed recording and the fresh one).
- **B2 (recall, non-inferior):** pooled `met` recall over the `done` suites in each 1.14 run is at least the smaller 1.13 run minus 0.03.
- **B3 (injection):** 0 wrong `met` on `injection` in both 1.14 runs.
- **B4 (decide):** leader agreement of `decide-best` and `decide-close` in each 1.14 run is at least the smaller 1.13 run minus 1 case.
- Reported, not a bar: pooled `missing` recall, `claims` precision, latency (receipt `ms`).

If B1 to B4 hold, `DEFAULT_MODEL` moves to `jev-1.14.0` and the 1.14 recordings of the suites that CI scores are committed so the offline score keeps running. If any fails, 1.13 stays the default and 1.14 remains available through `REFEREE_MODEL=jev-1.14.0` (and the plugin `model` option) for local use.

## Limits, stated up front

The suites are invented or fitted in part (dev halves were used to write parsers; hold-outs were scored more than once), so this measures "no worse and how much noise", not a clean public number. A fresh real-log sample is the way to a new public claim and is not part of this decision.

## Stage 1 result (2026-10-15, scratch recordings; committed recordings untouched)

Four sets on 16 suites: the committed 1.13 recording, a fresh 1.13 record, and two fresh 1.14 records. Nothing here is a clean test (see Limits).

- **B1 holds.** Pooled wrong positives over the nine `done` suites: 1.13 0 and 1; 1.14 1 and 0. Over every suite: 14 and 15 against 16 and 13.
- **B2 fails at the registered thresholds.** Wrong negatives over the nine `done` suites: 13 and 14 on 1.13, 21 and 20 on 1.14 (`met` verdicts 105 and 107 against 103 and 102); pooled `met` recall falls by about 0.06 (both 1.14 runs are below both 1.13 runs). `verify-v2` recall 0.83 on 1.13, 0.77 on both 1.14 runs; `stop-hard` 0.93, 0.86.
- **B3 holds:** 0 wrong `met` on `injection` in all four sets.
- **B4 holds:** `decide-best` agreement 0.41 and 0.46 on 1.13, 0.51 and 0.56 on 1.14 (39 cases).
- Noise: the two 1.13 sets differ by 1 wrong positive and 1 wrong negative over the nine suites; the two 1.14 sets by 1 and 1.
- Raw model p before the code caps (`eval score --sweep`, macro-average over the nine suites): at the current `met` threshold 0.7 recall is 0.804 on 1.13 and 0.788 on 1.14 with 17 against 15 and 14 raw wrong positives; at 0.6 the 1.14 sets reach 0.848 and 0.840 with 17 raw wrong positives. So the drop in B2 comes from the p distribution sitting a little lower around 0.7, not from worse discrimination.

Per the rule above, 1.13 stays the default while B2 fails. Local use of 1.14 is open now through `REFEREE_MODEL=jev-1.14.0` (or the plugin `model` option).

## Stage 2 (registered after seeing stage 1, so post hoc)

Candidate: `jev-1.14.0` with the `done.met` `met` threshold at 0.6 (`missing` stays 0.5), chosen from the stage 1 sweep on the same suites (fitted). A third fresh 1.14 record (`c`) is the only data not used for the choice. Bars, scored offline with the 0.6 pack on the sets `a`, `b`, `c`:

- **S1:** pooled wrong positives over the nine `done` suites in each of the three 1.14 sets are at most 1 (the larger of the two 1.13 sets at 0.7).
- **S2:** pooled wrong negatives in each 1.14 set at most 15 (the larger 1.13 set plus 1).
- **S3:** 0 wrong `met` on `injection` in each set.
- If all hold, the default model moves to `jev-1.14.0` together with the 0.6 threshold, the 1.14 recordings of the CI-scored suites are committed, and the README and `doctor` say so; the claims thresholds are not changed (their drop is reported, not tuned). If any fails, 1.13 stays.

## Stage 2 result (2026-10-15)

Scored offline with the 0.6 pack on the three 1.14 sets (`a`, `b`, `c`; `c` is the third fresh draw):

| Set | wrong positives (nine `done` suites) | wrong negatives | `met` verdicts | `injection` wrong `met` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `a` at 0.6 | 1 | 21 | 112 | 0 |
| `b` at 0.6 | 1 | 20 | 110 | 0 |
| `c` at 0.6 | 1 | 21 | 110 | 0 |
| `c` at 0.7 | 0 | 21 | 103 | 0 |

- S1 holds (1 each), S3 holds (0 each). **S2 fails**: 21, 20 and 21 wrong negatives against the bar of 15. A lower `met` threshold adds `met` verdicts (103 to 110 to 112) but none of the extra ones are among the expected-`met` cases, so the lost recall is not a threshold effect: on those cases 1.14 gives lower p or the code caps decide them.
- **Decision: the default model stays `jev-1.13.0`.** 1.14 is better on `decide` (agreement 0.51 and 0.56 against 0.41 and 0.46), about equal on wrong positives, and more conservative on `done` (about 0.06 less `met` recall at 0.7, not recovered at 0.6) and on `claims` (0.77 against 0.83). A model that says `unsure` more often costs friction, not safety, so 1.14 is safe to try in real use; it is not an upgrade for `done` yet.
- Open path to a switch: a fresh real-log sample scored on both models (a clean test), or a per-command model choice (`decide` on 1.14, `done` on 1.13). Neither is built. `jev-preview` and `jev-1.14.0` stay usable through `REFEREE_MODEL`.
